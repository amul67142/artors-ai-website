## Section 10 — FAQ

**How fast can we go live?**
Most systems are live within days. We start with a focused pilot so you see results quickly, then
expand from there.

**Do you only do voice agents and chatbots?**
No. Those are the most visible pieces, but a lot of our work is behind the scenes — connecting
systems, automating operations, generating content, and building the reporting that tells you
what's actually happening in your business.

**Do your AI agents work in Hindi and Hinglish?**
Yes. Our voice agents and chatbots handle English, Hindi and Hinglish — and are tested on real
Indian accents, phone audio and mixed-language conversation.

**Will this replace my staff?**
No — it frees them. AI handles the repetitive, high-volume work so your people focus on the work
that actually needs a human.

**What if the AI can't handle something?**
It hands off to your team cleanly, with full context, so the customer never has to repeat
themselves.

**Do you work with my industry?**
Almost certainly. The underlying systems are industry-agnostic — what changes is the workflow and
the vocabulary. Book a call and we'll tell you honestly if we're not the right fit.

**Do you only work with businesses in Gurugram?**
We're based in Gurugram and work with businesses across India. Everything is delivered remotely
and managed online.

**How much does it cost?**
It depends on what you're automating and the outcome you're after. Book a free strategy call and
we'll give you a clear, itemised scope — no surprises.

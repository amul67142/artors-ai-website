## 1. AI Automation Services
*Automate repetitive tasks and connect systems so clients save time and stop losing leads.*

### 1.1 Business Workflow Automation — *Connect your tools and eliminate manual work.*
Custom automation flows using n8n, Make and Zapier connecting CRM, WhatsApp, email, ads,
calendar and payments. Data flows automatically so the team stops copy-pasting.
**Deliverables:** custom workflows (unlimited steps) · CRM integration (Salesforce, HubSpot,
Zoho) · documentation and team training · 30 days post-launch support
**Best for:** any business losing time on manual data entry
**Price:** ₹35,000 – ₹2,00,000 per project

### 1.2 AI Sales & CRM Automation — *Never let a lead go cold again.*
End-to-end lead management: capture from any source, auto-qualify, assign to the right rep,
trigger follow-up sequences, update the CRM. Includes AI lead scoring.
**Deliverables:** multi-source capture (ads, website, WhatsApp, calls) · AI scoring and
auto-assignment · multi-channel follow-up sequences · CRM dashboard with real-time reporting
**Best for:** real estate, high-ticket B2B, coaching, healthcare
**Price:** ₹50,000 setup + ₹25,000–₹75,000/month

### 1.3 Custom AI Agent Development — *AI that thinks, decides, and takes action.*
Multi-step agents (LangGraph, CrewAI, OpenAI) that handle complete workflows autonomously —
research, drafting, sending, following up. Not a chatbot; an actual AI employee.
**Deliverables:** custom-trained agent · multi-tool integration (email, CRM, databases, web) ·
approval workflows for sensitive actions · ongoing training and optimisation
**Best for:** businesses ready to replace or augment a full-time role
**Price:** ₹1,50,000 – ₹8,00,000 per agent

## 2. AI Voice & Calling Services
*Human-sounding AI voice agents that call, qualify, and book meetings 24/7.*

### 2.1 AI Speed-to-Lead Calling — *Call every new lead within 60 seconds.*
The moment a lead arrives, the voice agent calls, greets by name, qualifies budget/timeline/
requirements, and books onto the rep's calendar. Hindi, English and regional languages.
**Deliverables:** agent trained on your product · multi-language · calendar integration · call
recordings, transcripts and analytics
**Best for:** real estate builders, insurance, education, healthcare
**Price:** ₹75,000 setup + ₹40,000–₹80,000/month

### 2.2 AI Outbound Calling Campaigns — *Reach thousands with a human-like AI voice.*
Lead reactivation, launches, feedback collection, event invites. Hundreds of simultaneous calls,
objection handling, interested contacts forwarded to the team.
**Deliverables:** custom script and objection handling · bulk calling (1,000+/hour) · live warm
transfer · campaign reports and conversion tracking
**Best for:** sales teams doing cold calling, reactivation, surveys
**Price:** ₹30,000 setup + per-minute usage or monthly retainer

### 2.3 AI Receptionist / Inbound Call Handling — *Never miss a call, even at 2 AM.*
Answers every inbound call, gives information, qualifies interest, books meetings or transfers
to a human. Sounds natural, handles interruptions.
**Deliverables:** 24/7 inbound handling · FAQ and information delivery · booking with calendar
sync · missed-call alerts and daily summaries
**Best for:** real estate offices, clinics, service businesses
**Price:** ₹40,000 setup + ₹25,000–₹50,000/month

## 8. Productized packages

Sell complete solutions rather than individual services — easier to sell, higher perceived
value, better margins. The source document frames this as "hiring an AI sales team".

| Package | Included | Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | WhatsApp AI bot + AI speed-to-lead caller + site-visit booking | ₹60,000 |
| **Growth** | Starter + ad funnel + landing pages + follow-up automation | ₹1,50,000 |
| **Enterprise** | Growth + broker portal + sales analytics + call auditing | ₹3,00,000 – ₹5,00,000 |

The framing works because buyers instantly understand the value of an "AI sales team" versus
buying individual tools, and it justifies premium pricing.

> Note: the package names above are written for real-estate builders. Under the general
> positioning they need renaming for a broader audience before they go on `/pricing`.

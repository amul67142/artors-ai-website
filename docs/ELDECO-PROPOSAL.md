# AI Calling & WhatsApp Nurturing
### A proposal for Eldeco, from Artors

---

## The problem this solves

Every enquiry Eldeco pays for — a form on 99acres, a Meta ad, a Google lead form —
arrives with a shelf life measured in minutes. The buyer who filled that form is
filling three more right now.

Whoever calls first usually gets the site visit. Not the best project, not the best
price. The first call.

Against that, a human presales team has a hard ceiling. They are asleep at 11pm when
a third of enquiries arrive. They are on another call when the next one lands. They
work Monday to Saturday, and leads do not.

The result is familiar to every developer: a spreadsheet of leads that were paid for
and never properly worked.

## What we propose

An AI presales system that calls every new enquiry **within a minute of it
arriving**, in Hindi, English or Hinglish, and nurtures the ones that do not answer
over WhatsApp until they do.

It qualifies against Eldeco's own criteria, books site visits into your sales
managers' calendars, and hands each qualified buyer to the right manager with a
summary of what was actually said.

Your team stops chasing and starts closing.

## How it works

```
Enquiry arrives  →  logged instantly
   (99acres, Meta, Google, website)
        │
        ├─ WhatsApp message, so the call is not from a stranger
        │
        └─ AI call within 60 seconds
                │
        ┌───────┴────────┐
    answered         no answer
        │                │
   qualified        WhatsApp: "we tried to reach you"
        │            + a link to pick a time
        │            retried twice, then handed to a human
        │
   site visit booked → calendar invite to buyer and sales manager
        │
   sales manager gets the number, the budget and the summary
```

Every call is recorded and transcribed. Every outcome lands in a sheet your team
can open without training.

## What Eldeco gets

**The system**
- AI voice agent, trained on Eldeco's projects and your own presales script
- Hindi, English and Hinglish, on one number
- WhatsApp nurturing for unanswered and undecided leads
- Automatic site-visit booking into your managers' calendars
- Every lead source connected: portals, Meta, Google, your website

**The visibility**
- A live sheet of every enquiry and what happened to it
- Every call recorded, transcribed and summarised
- Qualification captured on every call: budget, configuration, location, timeline,
  purpose, loan requirement
- Weekly review of what the agent got wrong, and a fix for each

**The handover**
- Your team trained on it
- Everything documented
- The system is yours

## What it does not do

Stated plainly, because a proposal that only lists strengths is not worth trusting.

- **It does not replace your sales team.** It reaches the lead first and finds out
  whether they are worth a manager's hour. Closing is still human work.
- **It does not quote prices or possession dates of its own accord.** It says only
  what Eldeco has approved in writing, and hands anything else to a person. In a
  RERA-regulated market that restraint is the point, not a limitation.
- **It cannot confirm unit availability.** Inventory moves faster than any system we
  are given access to.
- **It does not book during the call.** The buyer receives a booking link
  immediately afterwards. We would rather say this now than have you discover it in
  week three.

## What we suggest measuring

Judge this on one number: **site visits booked per hundred enquiries.**

Not calls made, not messages sent — those are activity, not outcome. We will also
measure your current response time in week one, so the comparison afterwards is
against your own baseline rather than against a claim from us.
